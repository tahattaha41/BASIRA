This policy describes the checks we expect before publishing an article or a game question.

Sources

Every important figure, date, and factual claim should be supported by a reliable source, such as scientific references, books, or official institutions. Sources should be listed so readers can check them. Disagreements between sources should be disclosed.

Review

An article or question should be reviewed by a person before publication. AI tools may help suggest titles or improve wording, but they do not publish content by themselves. Responsibility for accuracy remains with the publisher.

Updates and corrections

Information that changes over time should include a last-reviewed date. When a reported error is confirmed, we correct it and indicate the correction where appropriate.

Game questions

Each question should have one verifiable correct answer and a short explanation. We avoid questions based on personal opinion or disputed claims unless the uncertainty is clearly explained.

Advertising

If advertising is introduced, it must not influence editorial decisions or be placed where it could mislead readers while answering or navigating.
