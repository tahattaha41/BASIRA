BASIRA is a knowledge library paired with short brain games. Explore a sourced topic, then test what you learned in a few minutes—without creating an account.

What we offer

Articles cover science, history and civilizations, geography, technology, general knowledge, mathematics and logic, language and literature, and space. Five game formats are available: multiple choice, true or false, timeline ordering, memory, and word guessing.

How we work

We research and prepare articles, then review their information and sources before publication. We do not claim a review that has not happened, and we do not label an article verified until its references have been checked. Figures and dates should be supported by sources, with a review date when information can change.

What we do not do

We do not copy articles from other sites or publish repetitive material just to increase volume. If you find an error, contact us so we can investigate and correct it where needed.

Progress and accounts

You can use the site without an account. Game progress is currently stored only in your browser on this device and does not sync between devices. Optional account syncing is not available at this time.
