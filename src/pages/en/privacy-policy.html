Last updated: November 2026. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use BASIRA.

Information processed

The site runs on Google Blogger. Google may process technical visit information, such as browser details, pages viewed, and network identifiers, under its own policies. You do not need to provide personal information to read articles or play games.

Local storage and cookies

BASIRA stores your light/dark preference and game progress locally in your browser. This information remains on your device and is not sent to a BASIRA account or server. Google may use cookies to operate Blogger.

Accounts

User accounts are not currently offered by BASIRA. If this changes, this policy will be updated to explain the data stored and how it can be deleted.

Advertising

BASIRA does not currently display advertisements. If advertising is enabled later, this policy will be updated to explain relevant third-party practices and controls.

Children

The site is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact page.
