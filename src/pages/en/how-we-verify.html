This page explains how BASIRA handles information and the difference between sourced articles and games.

Choosing sources

We use sources readers can revisit, including encyclopedias, scientific institutions, official organizations, and published works. Articles should link to their sources; figures and dates must have supporting evidence.

Checking information

We compare claims, figures, and dates with the cited source. When sources disagree, we explain the disagreement instead of silently choosing one. Time-sensitive information should show when it was last reviewed.

What gets published

Articles are not meant to be published before review. We avoid thin pages created only to increase the number of posts.

Articles and games

Articles are the sourced reference material. Games help readers test their understanding. Questions are prepared and reviewed in advance, with an explanation, and are not generated during play.

Corrections

If you find an error, contact us with the article link and a reliable supporting source. We review reports and identify corrections when an article is changed.
