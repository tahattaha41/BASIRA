This page explains how BASIRA handles information and distinguishes sourced knowledge from game content.

Choosing sources

We prefer sources that readers can inspect, including scientific institutions, official organizations, reference works, and published research. Article references should link directly to the supporting source. We do not present a figure or date as verified when we cannot find supporting evidence.

Checking claims

Before publication, claims are compared with their cited sources. When credible sources disagree, the disagreement should be explained rather than silently choosing one version. Information that can change should include a review date.

Publication and review

Drafts are not treated as verified articles. Articles must be reviewed and their references checked before publication. We do not publish thin pages merely to increase the page count.

Articles and games

Articles are the reference material. Games help readers test what they understood. Game questions are prepared in advance and should include an explanation; they are not generated while a player is answering.

Corrections

If you find an error, contact us through the Contact page and include the article URL and a reliable source. We review reports and correct confirmed errors, noting material corrections where appropriate.
